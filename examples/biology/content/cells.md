# Cells

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of living organisms.
A cell membrane separates the interior of a cell from its surroundings and
helps regulate which substances enter and leave.

## Plant and animal cells

Plant and animal cells are eukaryotic cells. They contain a nucleus that holds
most of their genetic material and mitochondria involved in cellular respiration.

Plant cells have a cellulose cell wall outside the membrane. Many plant cells
contain chloroplasts, where photosynthesis takes place, and a large central
vacuole. Animal cells do not have a cellulose cell wall or chloroplasts.

## Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis uses light energy to help convert carbon dioxide and water into
sugars, releasing oxygen. In plants, this process takes place in chloroplasts.
The sugars can supply chemical energy and material for growth.
